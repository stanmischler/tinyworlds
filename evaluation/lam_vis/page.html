Zelda action space

Each point is one pair of frames four steps apart. Its position is the CoMo action model's continuous action for that pair (128 numbers), projected onto its first three principal components. Coloured points are held-out test pairs labelled by two vision-language judges; grey points are a random sample of training pairs. Hover a point to see its frames.
